Where it comes from

I've always said that my father is a different kind of beast. He barely has any leisure and always thinks of work or his family. For as long as I can remember, he was always doing something. He was always working.

Granted, he retired around when I was born, but he never stopped working. He taught me carpentry, and I worked with him. I think seeing him work so much inspired me, and I now see him as a role model. He passed down his love of working to me.

Building for the next generations. Although he built houses, I build automation software to give people their time back. I think it comes down to having a positive impact through hard work.
